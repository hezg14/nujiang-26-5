# Image credits & location verification

Every photograph on the site must pass two gates before it ships:

1. **Location gate** — it can be placed in Nujiang Prefecture from the source file's own
   record: a Commons category naming the county/township, an EXIF GPS fix inside the
   prefecture, or an author description naming the village. Filenames alone are not evidence.
2. **Licence gate** — a licence that permits commercial re-use with attribution (CC BY,
   CC BY-SA, CC0). Share-alike images are credited, and shared alike where redistributed.

Images that fail the location gate are either dropped or labelled **illustrative** in the
caption, and never given a Nujiang place name.

## Kongque Shan (孔雀山) — drawn as a profile, not faked with another mountain

Wikimedia Commons returns **zero** photographs of Kongque Shan or the Tianxin Twelve
Lakes (the query collapses to scanned books and unrelated "Peacock Mountain" hits).
Openverse and the commercial stock libraries block automated access from this environment,
and their Nujiang coverage is close to nil in any case.

The good Kongque Shan photographs that circulate — the winter ones especially — sit on
Chinese platforms (图虫, 视觉中国, 小红书, 怒江文旅 public accounts) and are
**all-rights-reserved**. Putting them on a public site without written permission is
infringement; "credit the source" is not a licence. So none was used.

Current handling: the card renders a **measured elevation profile** (4,400 m summit,
3,882 m pass, the lake basin, a 10 km scale) drawn in SVG. It is specific, it is honest,
and it reads as a specimen sheet rather than a stock photo. To upgrade it:

- supply 3–6 of your own photographs (best — you are local, and original frames also kill
  any stock-photo feel), or
- obtain written permission from 怒江州文旅局 / the photographer, then add the files here.

## Official-platform imagery

State and prefecture portals (nujiang.gov.cn, 云南网, 新华网) publish excellent Nujiang
photography, but their terms do not grant commercial re-use. They are used on this site as
**factual sources**, not as image sources. If you want their photos on the site, the route
is a written request to the publishing office — not a download.

## Correction, this pass: where Kongque Shan actually is

The card previously placed the peak in **兰坪县**. It is not there. Kongque Shan
sits on the **贡山县 / 德钦县 county line**, on the northern section of the Biluo
range, and the ridge it stands on is also the watershed between the Nujiang and
the Lancang. The 3,882 m pass is the high point of the old Degong road. Captions
and body copy in both dictionaries were corrected accordingly.

That correction also settles the Lanping photograph question: `A Mountain in the
Biluo Snow Mountain.jpg` (720 × 480, shot from Huangdeng dam in Lanping) is a
genuine Biluo frame, but it is the **southern** range, roughly 150 km from the
peak, and at 720 px it is too soft for a 380 px card at 2×. It is not used as a
stand-in for Kongque Shan, and `biluo-snow.jpg` (an unused earlier copy of the
same frame) was deleted from the repo.

The card is now a **dark specimen plate** rather than a pale chart: linear
elevation axis (2,000 / 3,000 / 4,000 m), summit crosshair at 4,400 m, the pass
notch at 3,882 m, lake dots on the saddle, a 10 km scale bar, and a corner tag
reading `DRAWN SECTION` so it never reads as a broken image. It is the only dark
card in a grid of photographs, which is what makes it look intentional.
